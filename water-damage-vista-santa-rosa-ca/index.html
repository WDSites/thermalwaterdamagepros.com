Steps to take while you wait

A rural address can mean a longer wait, so use the time to limit the damage.

  • Stop the source

    Close the valve nearest the leak, or the main valve if the source is unclear.

  • Keep power off wet areas

    If water touches outlets or the panel, do not enter. Call the utility or 911 first.

  • Protect the valuables

    Move rugs, books and electronics to dry, raised spots.

  • Open the gate

    Unlock gates and clear the driveway so the crew can reach the house.

  • Photograph everything

    Take dated photos and short video of the water and the damaged items.

  • Keep animals away

    Keep pets and livestock out of water that came from a drain or the outdoors.
