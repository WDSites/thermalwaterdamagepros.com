When water is spreading in Mecca

Mecca sits on the north shore of the Salton Sea in the eastern Coachella Valley, surrounded by farm fields. If water is spreading in a home in ZIP 92254, call (760) 769-1159 right away. The line never closes, and a crew goes out same-day when one is free.

In a farm community, a flooded irrigation line, a washing machine hose or a storm can put water in a house at any hour. The dispatcher will ask for the closest cross streets and the source of the water, then give you safety steps for the wait.

Harvest seasons, full households and water that runs for days

Mecca has about 8,200 residents, and roughly half of them work in agriculture. Up to 5,000 seasonal farmworkers arrive for winter and summer harvests. A household averages more than four people, and about 56 percent of homes are rented. More people in a house means more laundry, more showers and more strain on plumbing, and a leak under a sink can go unreported when everyone is working long shifts.

That pattern makes early detection harder. A damp cabinet or a soft floor near a bathroom is worth a call even when the stain looks small. If you rent, tell the landlord or manager at once, and ask them to contact their insurer.

If water is still spreading, do this first

These steps apply to a rented home, a family-owned house or a manufactured unit. Keep them simple.

  • Find the shutoff

    Shut the main valve or the fixture valve. If you do not know where it is, ask the landlord or manager now.

  • Stay out of live power

    If water touches outlets or appliances, do not enter. Call the utility or 911 first.

  • Move people and pets

    Keep children and pets away from water from a toilet, drain or outdoors.

  • Lift belongings

    Raise bedding, clothing and papers off wet floors and into a dry room.

  • Take dated photos

    Photograph the water and damaged items for your insurer or landlord before cleanup.

  • Call the 24/7 line

    Tell the dispatcher your cross streets and what is leaking.

Storms, canal water and the Salton Sea region

The eastern valley has a history of storms and flooding. A hurricane damaged the valley in 1976, and floods in the 1980s slowed development in the eastern valley. Irrigation here relies on groundwater and Coachella Canal water, which keeps farm fields green but also means water systems run close to homes.

The Salton Sea, North Shore and Arabia are nearby, though we mention them only as landmarks for directions. In heavy rain, ask your insurer whether you carry flood coverage, because standard homeowners policies typically exclude flood. The flood crew handles runoff, mud and contaminated water.

Once water is out, drying and moisture checks keep a wet wall from becoming a mold problem.

Mold after a leak in Mecca

Mold needs moisture, and a leak that was not noticed for a week gives it plenty. In a hot climate, closed windows and air conditioning keep moisture inside the house. If you see dark spots or smell something musty, our mold remediation team looks at the moisture source first and then plans containment.

We follow the practices in ANSI/IICRC S520 for mold, and we do not make health claims. Health questions belong to a physician.
