Water still spreading? Start here

These steps matter in a larger home, where a leak on one floor can reach rooms below.

  • Shut the main valve

    Find the main shutoff, often near the garage or the front of the house, and turn it off.

  • Watch the power

    Do not enter water near outlets, appliances or a wet panel. Call the utility or 911 first.

  • Protect finishes

    Lift rugs, art and small furniture. Do not drag wet furniture across hardwood.

  • Keep people out

    Keep children and pets away from water from a drain or from outdoors.

  • Photograph first

    Take photos of the water line and damaged items before anything is moved.

    Give your community, cross streets and the source of the water.

Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask your insurer whether flood coverage is part of your policy, since standard policies typically exclude it.

After a storm, the flood damage restoration crew treats incoming water as contaminated, removes debris and dries the structure. For a clean supply line leak, water mitigation is the faster path.

La Quinta is a city with its own building department. Ask the city's building department whether your repair needs a permit before major work begins.

Custom finishes and the repair or replace decision

If your home has hardwood floors, stone, custom cabinets or specialty paint, the repair plan changes. Hardwood can often be dried with mats and air movers before anyone decides to replace it. Stone and tile are often saved, and cabinets depend on what they are made of.

Matching finishes matters in a home where sight lines are long. We note which materials are original before work begins. California's Contractors State License Board says work worth $1,000 or more generally needs a licensed contractor, and you can check a license at cslb.ca.gov.
