Water Damage and Mold Services in Thermal, CA

Every stage of a water loss, from the first emergency call to the rebuild, handled by one team and one dispatch line.

How the services fit together

A water loss usually moves through four stages, and most homes need more than one. The emergency stage stops the water and pulls it out. The drying stage removes the moisture you cannot see. The cleanup stage removes damaged and contaminated material. The rebuild stage puts the walls, ceilings and floors back.

If water is still moving, start with emergency water removal for standing water or water mitigation for the drying plan. Call (760) 769-1159 any hour, because dispatch is open 24/7, and the dispatcher will tell you which service you need.

Which service matches your situation

These are the six core services, with the situation each one fits. More specific pages for causes, rooms and structures follow below the list.

Water damage repair fits a home that has dried and needs walls, ceilings, floors and paint rebuilt. It also covers ceiling and wall damage and cupped hardwood floors.

Flood damage restoration fits storm runoff, groundwater and sewage backups. Floodwater is treated as contaminated from the first minute, and low rooms and crawl spaces are part of it.

Water mitigation fits the first 24 to 72 hours after a leak, when drying equipment and moisture mapping stop damage from spreading. Structural drying is part of this service.

Emergency water removal fits standing water from a burst pipe, a failed appliance or a backed-up drain, where pumps and extraction units come first.

Water damage cleanup fits the removal of wet debris, the sanitizing of surfaces and the clearing of odors after the water is stopped.

Mold remediation is a secondary service for mold that follows a leak. It starts with the moisture source and moves to containment, removal and documentation.

What is not on the list

We do not handle waterproofing, foundation repair or plumbing repair. If water keeps coming into a basement or a crawl space, a drainage or waterproofing contractor solves that, and a plumber fixes the pipe itself. We will say so plainly during the call.
