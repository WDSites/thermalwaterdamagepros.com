Heat in summer, crowds in spring, storms when they come

Summers in Indio are extremely hot, so air conditioners run constantly. A clogged condensate line or a failing drain pan is a leading cause of ceiling and closet damage. A leak above a ceiling in August can drip for days before anyone notices.

Spring and winter bring festivals and visitors. The Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival runs at the Empire Polo Club, and the Riverside County Fair and National Date Festival fills the fairgrounds every February. Short-term rentals and guest properties cycle through guests quickly, so a plumbing leak between check-ins can go unreported. Dispatch is open around the clock, including event weekends.

When water is spreading in Indio

These steps work for a house, a rental or a small business. Do them only if they are safe.

  • Stop the water

    Shut the main valve. Rentals often have a shutoff in a utility closet or at the meter.

  • Do not touch live power

    If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

  • Move valuables up

    Lift electronics, papers and soft goods onto counters or into a dry room.

  • Keep people away

    Keep children and pets out of any water from a drain, toilet or outdoors.

  • Notify the owner

    If this is a rental or a guest property, notify the owner or manager and photograph the damage.

  • Call the 24/7 line

    Provide cross streets, the source and the water depth.

Power and water do not mix

Turn off the breaker only if the panel is dry. Otherwise stay out, call the utility and wait for the crew's instructions.

Housing, vacancy and water that runs unnoticed

Indio had about 34,600 housing units in 2020, and about 17 percent were vacant. Some of those are seasonal or second homes. A home that sits empty can leak for days or weeks before anyone knows. When someone finally opens the door, the damage can be much bigger than the original failure.

If you own a seasonal property, a smart habit is to know where the main shutoff is and to have someone check on it regularly. If you come home to wet floors or a musty smell, call us before you start moving things.

Storm water canals and the 2023 storm

Early Indio flooded more than once, and storm water canals were built across the valley to handle it. In 2023, Tropical Storm Hilary brought heavy rain, and Indio declared a local emergency. Even a desert city with canals can see water in streets and doorways when rain arrives fast.

After a storm, the flood damage crew treats incoming water as contaminated and removes mud and silt before drying. Ask your insurer about flood coverage, because standard homeowners policies typically exclude it. Check the FEMA flood map for your address.

Indio has its own city water department, the Indio Water Authority, and other providers serve parts of the valley. Check the name on your water bill so you call the right utility about a main line.
