North Shore is a census-designated place on the northeast shore of the Salton Sea in southeastern Riverside County, reached by State Route 111 and farther along the shore than Mecca. It uses ZIP code 92254, the same ZIP code as Mecca. If water is spreading through a North Shore home, call (760) 769-1159.

The county's Eastern Coachella Valley Area Plan describes North Shore as a resort community that is largely undeveloped, with pockets of residential and tourist-commercial uses. That means a house here may be a long way from its neighbors.

More homeowners, fewer neighbors and a longer wait

The 2020 Census counted about 3,600 residents and 882 housing units in North Shore, with about 94 percent occupied. Roughly three quarters of occupied homes were owner-occupied. If you own your home, you are usually the one who contacts the insurer and decides how to proceed.

First steps for a North Shore home

Use the wait to protect the house and everyone in it.

  • Close the valve

    Shut the fixture valve or the main valve if you can reach it safely.

  • Avoid wet electrical

    If water reaches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911.

  • Move people and pets

    Keep them out of water from a toilet, a drain or the outdoors.

  • Raise belongings

    Lift papers, bedding and electronics onto dry surfaces.

  • Take dated photos

    Photograph the water and damaged items before cleanup.

  • Call with details

    Give your street, the source and how deep the water is.

Electrical safety first

Do not use fans, vacuums or power strips while standing in water. Sparks or a gas smell mean you should leave the home and call 911.

The Salton Sea, storms and what is verified

The Salton Sea sits next to North Shore, but a nearby lake does not mean every street floods. A hurricane damaged the Coachella Valley in 1976, and floods in the 1980s hit the eastern valley. Those are valley-wide records, and we do not tie them to a single North Shore address.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified for North Shore. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows flood zones by address, and the California Department of Insurance notes that homeowners policies typically exclude flood. Your insurer can tell you what your own policy includes.
