Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Thermal, CA

A kitchen hides water in cabinet bases, under flooring and behind appliances, and we dry each of those spots before they turn into a bigger repair.

Call (760) 769-1159

A kitchen packs water lines, a drain, a dishwasher, a fridge and a lot of wood-based cabinetry into one small room. When a line lets go, the water runs under the cabinets and into the subfloor, where you cannot see it. This page walks through the kitchen one part at a time. For leaks that start at a dishwasher or fridge line, the page on appliance leak water damage goes into more depth.

Where a kitchen holds water

Most of the real damage sits in places a mop never reaches.

  • 1
    Toe-kicks and cabinet bases The thin panel at the floor hides the bottom of the cabinet, which sits on whatever is wet.
  • 2
    Particle-board boxes The pressed-wood cabinet frame swells when it soaks and rarely shrinks back.
  • 3
    Countertops and laminate edges Laminate peels where its core takes in water, and edges lift first.
  • 4
    Tile and the subfloor under it Grout lets water through, and the layer beneath can stay wet for days.
  • 5
    Supply lines behind cabinets Dishwasher and sink lines leak out of sight, so the first sign is often a soft floor.
  • 6
    Wall behind the range or fridge Drywall low on the wall wicks water upward and hides behind the appliance.

Safety steps before you touch anything

A wet kitchen is full of outlets and appliances, so go slowly.

  • 1
    Power Keep out of any puddle that reaches the range, fridge or an outlet. The breaker can be switched off only when the panel is dry and easy to reach, and a gas smell or sparking means the utility or 911 comes before our call.
  • 2
    Water Close the shut-off valve under the sink or behind the appliance. The main shut-off works if those do not.
  • 3
    Contents Lift food, small appliances and cookware out of wet cabinets and onto dry counters.
  • 4
    Photos Photograph the cabinets, floor and appliance before anything is moved.

Drying cabinets from the toe-kick

The first move is to pull the toe-kick and look at the base. That opens the cavity so a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera can read it, and so warm air from air movers reaches the underside. An extraction unit takes up water on the floor first, and LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the room air.

Cabinets can be dried when they are solid wood or plywood, the water was clean, and the finish and joints are still tight. They usually have to be replaced when the particle board has swelled, the laminate is bubbling or the water was contaminated.

Drying is checked over several days, which is typical, and the cabinet bases are tested again before anything is closed up. The industry reference for this work is ANSI/IICRC S500.

Floors under and around the kitchen

Tile is the sturdiest, but the thinset and subfloor under it hold water, so we check them with the meter instead of trusting the surface. Vinyl planks and sheet vinyl trap water beneath, and they are often lifted to dry the layer below. Laminate and hardwood swell at the seams and cup, and wet planks are often replaced.

Where the floor runs under cabinets, the dry area has to continue under them as well. This is the main reason a quick wipe-up is not enough, and why repair work should wait until readings are dry.

What sets the price of a kitchen job

You hear the scope and the price before anyone starts, and a few things shape both.

  • Water type, since clean water costs less to handle than contaminated water.
  • How many cabinet runs the water reached.
  • Floor type and whether the layer under it has to come up.
  • Whether the wall behind the range or fridge needs opening.
  • How long drying takes, which depends on materials and readings.

We do not repair plumbing, so a failed line needs a plumber, and cabinet work and plumbing worth $1,000 or more generally needs a licensed contractor.

Insurance and the claim for a wet kitchen

Insurers tend to separate sudden, accidental water from slow leaks. A supply line that bursts is often treated differently from a drip that has run for months. Ask your insurer which one applies, and read the California Department of Insurance residential property claims guide for how the process works.

Keep the swollen cabinet pieces until your adjuster has seen them. We record moisture readings and photographs so the claim has something solid to rest on. Flood is usually excluded and needs a separate policy.

Mistakes to avoid in a wet kitchen

Do not leave the toe-kick on and hope the cabinet dries. Do not seal wet cabinets with caulk or paint, and do not lay new flooring over a damp subfloor.

Avoid running the dishwasher or fridge while a leak is unresolved, and avoid running a household vacuum on wet floors.

La Quinta, Mecca and the permits behind a kitchen repair

About 73% of occupied homes in La Quinta are owner-occupied, according to Census figures. That means most kitchen decisions, such as keeping or replacing cabinets, belong to the homeowner and the insurer, not a landlord.

The city of La Quinta runs its own building department, while unincorporated Thermal and Mecca fall under Riverside County Building and Safety. Ask the building department that covers your address whether a repair needs a permit.

Summers bring many days above 110 degrees F, and a house with the cooling off during a leak can warm quickly, which speeds up odor and mold growth on wet wood. The EPA says mold can begin in 24 to 48 hours.
