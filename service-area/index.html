Service Areas Around Thermal, CA

Home base in Thermal, ZIP 92274, with crews covering four neighboring towns. Dispatch is open 24/7.

Where our crews respond

Our home base is Thermal, ZIP 92274, in Riverside County. From there, crews drive along State Route 86 and State Route 111 to the neighboring towns. Distances below are approximate, and the dispatcher confirms the route when you call (760) 769-1159.

Coachella, ZIP 92236, is about six miles northwest. It is the easternmost city in the valley and a farm and shipping hub, with a mix of newer homes and older trailer parks.

Mecca, ZIP 92254, is about seven miles southeast on the north shore of the Salton Sea. It is an agricultural community with large households and many rentals.

Indio, ZIPs 92201 and 92203, is about ten miles northwest. It is the valley's largest city, with festival weekends, seasonal homes and storm water canals.

La Quinta, ZIP 92253, is about twelve miles west-northwest. It sits beside the Santa Rosa Mountains and includes Old Town and PGA West.

Nearby places we mention for directions

The Salton Sea, North Shore and Arabia sit near Mecca, and Palm Springs lies west of Indio. We use them as reference points when a caller needs directions, and they are not separate service pages. If your property is in one of these places, call and the dispatcher will confirm coverage.

The Torres-Martinez Desert Cahuilla Indians have a reservation in the Thermal area. If you are unsure whether your address is in our coverage, ask the dispatcher.

What to have ready when you call

Give the dispatcher your cross streets, the nearest highway, and what the water is doing. Tell them where it came from if you know, and whether anything electrical is wet. For a rural address, a nearby landmark helps.

Every area page explains what is different about that town, from housing to storms. Start with the one closest to you, then read water mitigation for the drying process.
