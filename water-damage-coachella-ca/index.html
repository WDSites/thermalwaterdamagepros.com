Newer homes, older parks, and the water problems each one has

Coachella was incorporated in 1946, but a large share of its housing is much newer. Many single-family homes and apartment complexes were built after 2000. In homes of that age, the usual culprits are supply line fittings, water heaters, refrigerator lines and air conditioner condensate drains. They fail quietly, and the damage shows up on a ceiling below or a wall that feels cool.

The city also has older trailer parks, and in some outlying areas city water and sewer lines may not reach every property. Manufactured homes are built differently from site-built houses, and water can reach the subfloor quickly. Where a property is on private water or sewer service, a backup or main line failure involves your plumber and your provider first, and we handle the water inside.

When water is spreading in Coachella

Start these steps while you wait for the crew. They apply to a leak in a house, a rental unit or a manufactured home.

  • Shut off the water

    Turn off the main valve or the fixture valve. In a manufactured home the main shutoff may be outside, near the skirting.

  • Check the electricity

    If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 before calling us.

  • Lift valuables

    Move electronics, papers and small furniture off wet floors and into a dry room.

  • Keep people away

    Keep children and pets out of water that came from a drain, toilet or outdoors.

  • Tell your landlord

    If you rent, tell the landlord or manager right away and keep photos with dates.

  • Call the 24/7 line

    Give the dispatcher your cross streets, the source and how deep the water is.

Electrical safety first

Cut power at the panel only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping through water. If you see sparks or smell gas, leave and call 911.

Where Coachella homeowners and renters usually need us

The first call is usually a burst or failed supply line, a water heater leak or an overflowing washer. That points to water mitigation first, where crews map moisture and set drying equipment. A kitchen or bathroom leak that has been running for days is a different call, because the wall behind it may need to open.

Farm and packing operations drive a lot of Coachella's economy, with employers that include the Coachella Valley Unified School District, nearby tribal casinos, Valley Pride and the Coachella Valley Water District. Shift workers get leaks at night and on weekends, so after-hours response matters. Same-day response is available when crews are free, and the dispatcher will say plainly what to expect.

For renters, a leak can involve a landlord, a property manager and an insurer. Document everything and ask the manager for the policy details before work begins.
